- Compute `mean@16`, `mean_parsed`, `maj@16` (`maj_tie_wrong`, `maj_tie_random`, `maj_tie_correct`), `pass@16`, `stable_12`, `entropy`, `mean_new_tokens`, and the 15-subject Declarative (`8` high-$a^{\text{pop}}$ domains) vs. Procedural (`6` low-$a^{\text{pop}}$ quantitative domains) log-odds gains $\overline{\Delta L}_m$.
- **Rule 1 (Operator Composition: `M1b` against `t9_sft` and `q1_grpo`)**:
  - **`+1 (Constructive Operator Composition)`**: `M1b` beats both `t9_sft_<seed>_70` (`0.439`) AND `q1_grpo_<seed>` (`0.469`) on `mean@16` with $d_s > 0$ in `3 of 3` seeds and mean $d > 2.92\text{ SE}_d$ against both baselines.

(`pass@16` is reported as description beside `maj@16`, not as a second trigger of branch `-1`.)
  - **`0 (Null / Bounded by Standard RL)`**: Any other outcome (for example, `M1b` rises above `t9_sft_<seed>_70` on `mean@16` to tie `q1_grpo_<seed>` within `2.92 SE_d` without meeting `+1` or `-1`).
- **Rule 2 (Token Temperature `M1a` vs. Trajectory Concentration `M1b`)**:
  - `M1b` beats `M1a` (`T = 0.2`, zero training) if $d_s(\text{M1b} - \text{M1a}) > 0$ on `mean@16` in `3 of 3` seeds with mean $d > 2.92\text{ SE}_d$; `M1a` suffices on cost (`-1`) if `M1a` is within `0.01` of `M1b` (`mean d(M1a - M1b) - 2.92 SE_d > -0.01`) and beats `t9_sft_<seed>_70` by $>2.92\text{ SE}_d$; otherwise `0`.
- **Rule 3 (Psychometric ZPD Item Targeting `M1c` vs. Unfiltered `M1b`)**:
  - Within-model ZPD item targeting helps training (`+1`) if `M1c` beats `M1b` on `mean@16` with $d_s > 0$ in `3 of 3` seeds and mean $d > 2.92\text{ SE}_d$; hurts (`-1`) if `M1c` is below `M1b` in `3 of 3` seeds with mean $d < -2.92\text{ SE}_d$; otherwise `0` (the natural $G = 5$ zero-variance shutoff in `M1b` already suffices).
