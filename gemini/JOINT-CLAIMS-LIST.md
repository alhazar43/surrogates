> ### Global RL Numerical Caveat: `bf16` Master-Weight Rounding Limit (`repo_s0/rl.py:362, 380–383`)
> The RL training implementation (`repo_s0/rl.py`, shared across `T9`, `Q1`, and `M1`) stores model parameters directly in `bfloat16` with **no `float32` master copy**. At learning rate $\eta = 5\times 10^{-6}$, standard `bf16` stochastic rounding discretization discards any gradient update for weights with magnitude $|w| \gtrsim 2^{-9} \approx 0.00195$.
> - **Empirical Verification on `q1_grpo_0` (after 333 training steps)**:
>   - `model.norm.weight`: **$0$ of $2,048$ entries changed** ($100\%$ frozen by rounding).
>   - Layer 8 `down_proj`: only **$10.2\%$ of entries changed**.
>   - Layer 8 `q_proj`: only **$7.9\%$ of entries changed**.
>   - **Maximum changed entry**: $|w| \le 0.00195$ across all inspected tensors.
> - **Scope & Consequence**: All RL empirical readings in this study (the flat `Q1` critic arms, the `47.9%` `Mean@16` ceiling, GRPO slope sharpening $\tau_s$, $4\times$ option-order dispersion amplification $D$, and cross-seed item residuals $\kappa_{sj}$) operate under this rounding limit. Whether these dynamical phenomena change under unconstrained `float32` master weights is currently untested.
