## 3. Methodological Confounds Uncovered in Pre-Oct20 Original Units

Even inside the untouched pre-Oct20 repositories (`signature`, `irt4llm`, `irt4llm-standalone`), our raw-code and synthetic-gate audit uncovered **7 critical methodological confounds** that must be respected whenever citing those units:

### 3.1 `signature/T8` (`F30`): Peaked-Gain / Negative $\rho(\Delta p_i, p_{\text{base},i})$ Is Confounded by Ceiling Compression & Accuracy Loss
- **What `T8` Reported**: Across 47 fine-tuned states on `Qwen3-4B`, $\Delta p_i$ correlates negatively with $p_{\text{base},i}$ (mean Spearman `-0.499`, $\Delta\text{logit}$ `-0.798`), and 64/112 trained-subject rows break rank order ($z < -2$).
- **Three Verified Confounds (`results/T8/runs/synthetic.csv`, `T1`, `T5`, `notes/state.md:153`)**:
  1. **Mechanical ceiling compression**: `T8`'s own `synthetic.csv` shows that a pure rank-preserving monotone shift of $+2$ logits produces a negative Spearman between $\Delta p_i$ and $p_{\text{base},i}$ (median **`-0.183`**, 5th percentile **`-0.403`**) because items near $p_{\text{base}} \approx 1$ have no room to rise.
  2. **Accuracy-loss / wrong-key regime**: In `T1` and `T5`, **all 47 fine-tuned states LOST accuracy on their trained subject** (because base `Qwen3-4B` started at `0.73–0.87` accuracy, and 20 of the 35 `T5` states were trained on a shifted wrong key!). When accuracy drops or labels are flipped, items where the base model was initially right ($p_{\text{base},i} \approx 1$) fall the most.
  3. **Panel Withdrawal**: The internal panel (`signature/notes/state.md:153`) formally withdrew the item-order/peaked-gain route marks after four derivations confirmed mechanical confounds.
- **What Survives in `T8`**: **`T8-P3`**—that non-target OOD damage correlates with Forward KL from base on the trained subject at Spearman **$\rho = 0.795$** (`T5`) and **$\rho = 0.870$** (pooled $N=46$)—is `[VERIFIED-RAW]` and `[SOLID FACT]`.

### 3.2 `signature/U2` (`F12`): Residual-Stream Jacobian Push Has In-Sample Overlap on $N_j$ & Asymmetric Null
- **What `U2` Reported**: Pushing `Qwen3-8B`'s residual stream along $+v_j$ correlates with the human student NRM pattern $\tilde{\lambda}^\top d_j$ at $r = 0.389$ ($p = 0.000$) for cluster `26`, $r = 0.140$ for `124`, and $r = 0.072$ for `43` at layer 27.
- **Code-Level Confound Discovered (`src/signature/u2.py:265-275, 353-405` & `push.py:169-175`)**:
  1. **No Train/Test Split on $N_j$**: For each error name $j$, the push direction $v_j \propto \sum_{(q,o)\in N_j} g_{q,o}$ is the mean option-logit gradient over **all kept $(q,o) \in N_j$**. The resulting logit shift $\Delta\ell_{q',o'}$ is then correlated with $\tilde{\lambda}_{q',o'}^\top d_j$ across **all 744 items including $N_j$ itself**. Pushing along $\sum_{(q,o)\in N_j} g_{q,o}$ mechanically boosts the exact options $(q,o) \in N_j$ whose gradients were summed!
  2. **Asymmetric Permutation Null (`U2-notes.md:39`)**: Each permutation replicate permutes distractor labels to build $v_j^{\text{perm}}$ (boosting a random set $N_j^{\text{perm}}$) but correlates the shift against the **unpermuted** student pattern $\tilde{\lambda}^\top d_j$ (which spikes on $N_j$). Thus the real test enjoys direct in-sample index overlap on $N_j$ that the permuted null lacks.
- **What Survives in `U2`**: **`F15` (Layer of Formation)**—that `Qwen3-8B`'s response signature reaches $r \ge 0.9$ with final output at **Layer 24–26** (out of 36) under both J-lens and logit lens—is clean (`[SOLID FACT]`). Do **not** cite `F12` as out-of-sample causal steering unless re-run with leave-one-item-out or split-half $N_j$.

### 3.3 `signature/U1g & U1k` (`F10, F17`): Student Cross-Item Error Transfer Is Driven by Topic Factors & 88% Sparsity
- **What `U1g/U1k` Reported**: 11 of 61 named error directions transfer across item halves ($P_j \to Q_j$) under Laplace-corrected scoring (`U1k`).
- **Two Verified Caveats (`findings.md F10, F17`, `results/U1k/transfer.json`)**:
  1. **88.1% Sparsity**: `88.1%` of single-name student reads have Fisher information $< 4$ (`absent`), and pooled signature gain over 1D $\theta$ across the 14 passing names is only **`+0.00082` nat/answer** (vs **`+0.03421`** for full 5D $\xi$).
  2. **Topic-Factor Confound (`F10`)**: Inspecting the items inside the top transferring VLM clusters shows that Cluster `43` is angle calculation across 73 geometry items, Cluster `4` is coordinate plane, and Clusters `18`/`26` are generic arithmetic catch-alls. Thus cross-item transfer on static item splits reflects **sub-topic ability factors** (geometry, coordinates), whereas **`U1m/U1n` (adjacent-quiz temporal persistence with an `8.24`-quiz half-life)** is the genuine `[HIDDEN GEM]`.

### 3.4 `signature/T10` (`F34`) & `item-params-llm/S1` (`M9`): Withdrawn Item-Drift & Mode-Reorder Counts
- **`T10` (`signature/results/T10/runs2/synthetic_mirt_summary.csv`)**: After fixing Attempt 1's double-BOS bug (`0.380 -> 0.441` base accuracy) and replacing custom Python EM with R `mirt 1.48`, the 2PL item-drift rule failed its synthetic global scale-flattening null (`65%–100%` false-positive rate at $c = 0.67$) and had near-zero power on planted item drift. **Item-side drift in `T10` was formally withdrawn (`findings.md:60`)**.
- **`S1` Mode Reordering (`item-params-llm/results/closure.md:40, 43`, `review-2026-10-19/M.md M9`)**: Excess $k=16$ mode change over the symmetric bootstrap null held in only `1 of 3` seeds (`0.130–0.137` SFT vs `0.112–0.145` GRPO) and was proved by Reviewer M (`M9`) to be mechanically confounded with distribution sharpness. **Formally withdrawn**.

### 3.5 `plms-recent/irt4llm/P2` vs. `P2b` & `E1`: Uncentered RDM Leak (`P2`) & 61-LLM NRM Slope Failure (`E1`)
- **`P2` Withdrawn, Replaced by `P2b`**: Uncentered option RDM (`P2`, $\rho = 0.165$) failed its synthetic `shared_difficulty_only` gate ($\rho = 0.153$) because first-order option popularity leaked into the grand-mean correlation. **`P2b`** fixed this by centering each $(q, o)$ cell across respondents of each kind (`shared_difficulty_only` dropped to `0.020`, real $\rho = \mathbf{0.1477}, p = 0.0$). **Always cite `P2b`, never `P2`**.
- **`E1` / `closure.md` Withdrawal of 61-LLM Fitted NRM Parameters**: At $R = 61$ respondents, neither `mirt` nor `nrm_fit` recovers nominal item slopes ($r = 0.729\text{–}0.855 < 0.90$, and `mirt` suffers pattern-likelihood float64 underflow above 520 items). All claims resting on NRM parameters fitted *on* the 61 LLMs (`P1a–P1f`) were withdrawn in `closure.md`, whereas scoring the 61 LLMs on the **fixed 4,918-student calibration** (`E1`, `P4`) and the **model-free RDM (`P2b`)** stand intact.

### 3.6 `plms-recent/irt4llm/P3 & P5` and `signature/T2`: Early Pretraining Format/Reliability Confound
- **On Eedi (`P3`, `P5`)**: 14 of 26 `OLMo-2-1124-7B` checkpoints have A–D letter mass $< 0.5$ (`step150` mass `0.0022`, scoring the uniform prior), 26/26 misfit the student map ($l_z \le -13.5$), and after `step35000` (first checkpoint fitting the model map), student-map $\Delta\theta = +0.156$ is below the low-mass noise floor (`0.204`).

- On the **7 seen `S1` models**, 2PL $\hat{\theta}$ has higher $|d/\text{SE}|$ than accuracy in `3/3` SFT-vs-GRPO pairs (`8.63–10.49` vs `3.79–5.87`) and flips credit attribution from `60% SFT / 40% GRPO` (accuracy) to `21% SFT / 79% GRPO` ($\hat{\theta}$).
- **However**, `S1` was already seen when Rule 2 was written, and the pre-registered unseen test on the **12 `Q1` models** failed (`2/6` pairs, because 5 of the 6 `Q1` pairs did not differ in accuracy; on the 1 pair that differed, `pop1_2 vs pop0_2`, $\hat{\theta}$ was sharper at $z=5.11$ vs $3.69$). Consequently, `results/report-q1q2.md:79` **formally withdrew the `S1` reading of Rule 2 as a confirmed finding**. Whenever showing the `S1` attribution reversal or $|d/\text{SE}|$ ratio, explicitly state that it is on the **7 seen `S1` models** and explain the exact mathematical reason ($a_j^{\text{pop}}$ weighting).

### 3.8 `item-params-llm/Q2g` Reading A (`Q2-A`): Both Rules Carry the Common Gain $(\tau_s - 1)\mu_{0j}$ & Shared Base Sampling Error
- **What `Q2-A` Reported**: Top-10% item-change reproducibility across seeds is `0.524 to 0.789` for the IRT residual and `0.490 to 0.782` for the classical change (differences `-0.041 to +0.075` across 36 seed pairs in `results/Q2g/s1/reproducibility.csv` and `q1/reproducibility.csv`, against `0.10` for random sets).
- **Methodological Caveat (`plan-within-run.md §2.2`, `math.md §1.6`)**: Both rules retain the dominant common gain term $(\tau_s - 1)\mu_{0j}$ AND share the exact same Base sampling error $e_{0,j}$ across seeds. Therefore, `Q2-A` did **not** test whether genuine item-level change $\kappa_{sj}$ repeats across seeds beyond location $\delta_s$ and common gain $\tau_s$. Testing item-level change requires splitting the Base counts into independent option-order halves (one half per seed) and removing the common gain $\tau_s$ in the latent-logit likelihood (**`Row R1 / Prediction P1`** in `plan-within-run.md`).
