## 3. Classification Tags Used Across This Knowledge Base

Every empirical claim and mathematical proposition is tagged on three orthogonal axes:
1. **Provenance**:
   - `[ORIG-PRE-OCT20]`: Executed and recorded by the user's original multi-agent system prior to Oct 20, 2026.
   - `[POST-OCT20-CLAUDE]`: Audited or executed by Claude (`CC-bot`) on/after Oct 20, 2026.
   - `[POST-OCT20-GEMINI]`: Proposed or authored by Gemini on/after Oct 20, 2026.
2. **Verification Level**:
   - `[VERIFIED-RAW]`: Numbers directly recomputed from raw `.csv` / `.json` files on local disk via Python verification scripts.
   - `[VERIFIED-MATH]`: Analytical derivation checked step-by-step against independent review (`*-review.md`, `review-2026-10-19/M.md`, or `identification.md`).
   - `[DOC-ONLY / CLUSTER-RAW]`: Recorded in committed pre-Oct20 markdown reports (`irt4llm-standalone`), but raw arrays live only on the cluster.
3. **Scientific Status**:
   - **`[HIDDEN GEM]`**: High-leverage, large-effect (`>> 0.01`), non-trivial empirical or mathematical discovery that survived all synthetic controls and caveats.
   - **`[SOLID FACT]`**: Clean, verified empirical baseline or negative result that holds under its pre-registered test.
   - **`[CONFOUNDED / CAVEATED]`**: Real number on disk, **but** carries a critical methodological confound (e.g., ceiling compression, in-sample overlap, base-difficulty collinearity, or seen-state selection) that **must** be disclosed or disqualifies naive interpretation.
   - **`[NULL / NEGATIVE]`**: Pre-registered test executed and cleanly falsified.
   - **`[OVERSELL — WITHDRAWN]`**: Post-hoc spin, invented terminology, or misquoted number that has been permanently retracted.
