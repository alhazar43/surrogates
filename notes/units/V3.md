### Specification
Definitions, from the author. A probe is a question sent at a problem that cannot be observed directly. A surrogate is a measurable quantity that stands in for a probe's exact target, used because the exact target cannot be computed with the data or the model at hand. Each probe has several surrogates.
1. Copy version 2 to program/archive/research-program-v2.md.
2. Keep sections 1 and 2 short. Restate the question and the two definitions above.
3. Write one entry per probe of the sweep files, with these eight fields:
   - Exact target, one sentence.
   - Bet, one sentence a reader can lose.
   - Surrogates, at least two, taken only from the sweep files or from theory files where they were checked. For each, give its name, source and the way it misleads. Invent none.
